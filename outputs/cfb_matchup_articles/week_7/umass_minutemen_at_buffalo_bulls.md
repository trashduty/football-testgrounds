## Our Take

The UMass Minutemen visit the Buffalo Bulls at UB Stadium. We make **Buffalo -7**, compared with a market line of -3.5.

The best number we found is Buffalo -3.5 at -110. We give Buffalo a 55.5% chance to cover, which creates an 3.1% edge for us. That clears our 3% threshold, so Buffalo is a bet.

Our view starts with Buffalo’s defense against the UMass running game. Our numbers rate Buffalo’s run defense as the strongest unit in this matchup, while UMass has struggled to generate efficient rushing offense. That matters because limiting the run can keep UMass from controlling the game and place more responsibility on a passing attack that our numbers also rate below average.

Buffalo’s own rushing profile is the less convincing part of the case. The Bulls produced 302 rushing yards against Robert Morris, including a career-high 146 from Messiah Burch, but we are not treating one performance as proof that the broader efficiency issues are fixed. Buffalo does at least have another way to create points: Chance Morrow accounted for all six of the team’s receiving touchdowns through four games.

The meaningful concern for us is Buffalo’s tendency to create unnecessary volatility. The Bulls committed nine turnovers through four games, losing six of nine fumbles, and they were outscored 45-6 in first quarters. Those issues make separation less comfortable, but the underlying matchup—particularly Buffalo’s ability to restrict the UMass running game—is enough to support our position.
