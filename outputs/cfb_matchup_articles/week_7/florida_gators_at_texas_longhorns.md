## Our Take

The Florida Gators visit the Texas Longhorns at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium. We make **Florida +11**, compared with a market line of +10.

The best number we found is Florida +10.5 at -115. We see a -1.4% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

We view Florida’s win over Ole Miss as useful context, not proof that the same performance carries over. Florida rushed for 302 yards while allowing no sacks, and Aaron Philo has completed 63 of 87 passes with seven touchdowns against two interceptions through four games. That balance gives Florida a credible way to sustain drives and remain competitive rather than relying entirely on turnovers or explosive plays.

The concern for us is Texas’ passing game against a Florida defense that has allowed 248.3 passing yards per game. Arch Manning has seven touchdown passes through three games, while Cam Coleman already has four receiving scores. Our matchup numbers identify this as the clearest area where Texas can create separation, even though Florida’s pass rush recorded eight sacks across its first two SEC games.

That tension explains why our view stays close to the market. Florida’s offensive efficiency makes the underdog interesting, but the potential Texas advantage through the air keeps us from finding enough value at the available price. For us, this remains a pass.
