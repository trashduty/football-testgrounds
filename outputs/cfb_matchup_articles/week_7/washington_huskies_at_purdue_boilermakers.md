## Our Take

The Washington Huskies visit the Purdue Boilermakers at Ross-Ade Stadium. We make **Washington -0.5**, compared with a market line of +3.

The best number we found is Washington +3.5 at -114. We give Washington a 58.6% chance to cover, which creates an 5.3% edge for us. That clears our 3% threshold, so Washington is a bet.

Our view starts with Purdue’s offense against Washington’s defense. Our longer-run numbers show a major Washington advantage in rush efficiency, and they also favor Washington in preventing scoring opportunities. The early results fit that profile without proving it: Washington allowed 289.3 yards and 16.8 points per game through four games, while opponents converted only 29 percent of their third downs. For us, that makes it difficult to price Purdue as the clearly stronger side.

The more interesting part is that Washington also owns the better scoring-opportunity matchup on the other side. Demond Williams Jr. had thrown for 1,129 yards and rushed for 165 through four games, giving Washington multiple ways to move the ball. The concern is protection: Minnesota held Washington to 40 rushing yards and recorded seven sacks, while Purdue produced three sacks and eight tackles for loss in its opener. We are not ignoring that volatility, but our case is less about Washington controlling every snap and more about the matchup giving Washington enough paths to outperform the market’s expectation.
