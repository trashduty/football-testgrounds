We also see encouraging early stability around Devon Dampier, who produced 690 passing yards with seven touchdowns and one interception through three games. We view that combination as more meaningful than the headline scoring totals alone.

Houston’s rushing profile and scoring-opportunity creation therefore make this less comfortable than the early results might suggest. Our broader rating still favors Utah more than the market does, but that disagreement is not large enough to overcome the available price, so we are passing.
