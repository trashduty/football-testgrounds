The best number we found is Tennessee +7.5 at -106. We see a 2.7% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

We are not treating Alabama’s early offensive production as proof that it will create separation here. That disruption and pass-defense strength matter against Keelon Russell, who completed 71.2 percent of his passes through his first five starts.

Tennessee also has protection concerns around true freshman Faizon Brandon after allowing 13 sacks through four games, including 10 against Texas. For us, the case for Tennessee is primarily about its defense limiting Alabama’s passing game, but the conflicting trench matchups leave too little value to move us off the pass.
