The best number we found is Charlotte +22.0 at -109. We see a 2.2% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our model is less willing than the market to price Temple for comfortable separation. We understand the favorite’s case: Temple has produced at least 176 rushing yards in each of its first three games and ran for 270 yards against Toledo. That profile matters against a Charlotte defense that grades poorly in both rushing efficiency and scoring-opportunity prevention.

The more interesting part for us is whether Temple can turn that offensive advantage into sustained margin. Temple allowed 297.3 passing yards per game through three contests, while opponents converted 51.3 percent of their third downs. Those issues leave room for Charlotte to extend possessions and stay within range even if Temple controls stretches of the game. We see enough to lean toward Charlotte, but Temple’s favorable offensive matchup keeps the disagreement too small for us at the current price.
