### The Matchup To Watch

The matchup we are watching is Army's rushing offense against Tulsa's defensive front. Tulsa used the same front-seven starters in each of its first four games, with Chris Thompson Jr. and Dax Collins leading the team in tackles. That continuity and early run-defense production provide a meaningful test for Army's experienced offensive line.

We think Tulsa's ability to limit productive early-down runs will shape the game. If Army consistently stays on schedule, its multidimensional ground game can control possessions; if Tulsa creates longer-yardage situations, the market's preference for the home team becomes easier to justify.
