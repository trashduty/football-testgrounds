## Our Take

The Bowling Green Falcons visit the Buffalo Bulls at UB Stadium. We make **Buffalo -6**, compared with a market line of -3.

The best number we found is Buffalo -3.5 at +100. We give Buffalo a 53.5% chance to cover, which creates an 3.5% edge for us. That clears our 3% threshold, so Buffalo is a bet.

For us, the case is less about Buffalo being a complete team and more about Bowling Green's ability to sustain enough offense. Our longer-run numbers remain skeptical of Bowling Green's rushing attack, which averaged 107.3 yards through six games as part of an offense producing 275 total yards per game. The 225-yard rushing performance against Sacramento State showed a higher ceiling, but we are not treating one season-best outing as a new baseline.

Buffalo's clearest path is also on the ground. Its 302 rushing yards against Robert Morris were a season high, while Bowling Green had allowed 204.5 rushing yards per game through six contests. That alignment helps explain why our expectation is more favorable to Buffalo than the market's. The meaningful concern is Bowling Green's disruptive front: it generated 25 tackles for loss and 15 sacks over its three most recent games, and Buffalo entered the season with all five projected offensive-line starters set to make their first career starts for the program. We still think Buffalo can limit that exposure by leaning on the more favorable rushing matchup.
