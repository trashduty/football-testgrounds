Our model’s case for Memphis starts with offensive flexibility. We rate the Memphis passing game well above Army’s pass defense, and the rushing matchup also favors the home offense. That matters because Memphis does not appear dependent on one specific path to create separation; our numbers suggest it can challenge Army through either phase.

The counterweight is Army’s rushing offense against a Memphis run defense that has graded poorly. We see a credible path for Army to sustain drives and reduce the number of possessions, which is especially relevant when evaluating a favorite.
