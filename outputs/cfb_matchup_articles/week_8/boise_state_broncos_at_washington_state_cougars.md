### The Matchup To Watch

We are watching Washington State’s passing offense against Boise State’s pass defense. Washington State allowed 13 sacks in its first four games, including six against Arizona, and Boise State owns the stronger efficiency profile in this phase. If that disadvantage consistently creates negative plays, Washington State will have difficulty staying within the number.

Pinnick’s mobility is the counterweight for us. Through four games, he had 13 runs of at least 10 yards, tied for third-most among quarterbacks nationally. We do not expect that alone to erase the matchup disadvantage, but his ability to create outside the structure gives Washington State another way to extend drives and makes Boise State’s path to separation less straightforward.
