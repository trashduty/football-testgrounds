## Our Take

The Oregon Ducks visit the Illinois Fighting Illini at Memorial Stadium (Champaign, IL). We make **Oregon -11**, compared with a market line of -8.5.

The best number we found is Oregon -8.5 at -110. We see a -0.5% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our numbers are more favorable to Oregon than the market because we see multiple ways for its offense to sustain drives and create separation. The passing matchup stands out, with Illinois grading much worse against the pass than Oregon does throwing it. Dante Moore has nine touchdown passes without an interception through three games, and Oregon has converted 13 of 14 red-zone opportunities into touchdowns. That combination matters when laying points because empty possessions can quickly erase a favorite’s margin.

We also see an advantage in Oregon’s ability to prevent scoring opportunities. Illinois has not consistently created those chances, while Oregon’s defense grades well at keeping opponents away from the high-value areas of the field. The concern for us is that Oregon allowed more than 400 total yards and 200 rushing yards in its loss at Oklahoma State, so we cannot treat the defensive profile as settled.

That is why this remains a pass. We understand why our model makes Oregon a larger favorite, but the current price already reflects much of the matchup advantage. Our disagreement with the market is real, just not valuable enough at the available number.
