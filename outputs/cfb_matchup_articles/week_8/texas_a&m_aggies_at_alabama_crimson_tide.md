### The Matchup To Watch

We are watching Marcel Reed against Alabama’s pass defense. Reed supplied 236 passing yards and a team-high 80 rushing yards against Kentucky, so his mobility gives Texas A&M a way to extend possessions when the initial play breaks down. Alabama, however, pairs an experienced secondary with a run defense that allowed 78.6 yards per game through five contests.

We think that combination can place more of the game on Reed’s passing. Texas A&M generated only nine plays of at least 20 yards through three games, so the question is whether Reed can create enough explosive offense without exposing Texas A&M to the turnovers that have fueled Alabama’s scoring margins.
