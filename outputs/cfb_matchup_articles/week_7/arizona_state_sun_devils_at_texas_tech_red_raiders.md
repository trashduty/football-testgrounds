### The Matchup To Watch

We are watching Arizona State’s passing game against Texas Tech’s scoring-opportunity prevention. If the Sun Devils cannot run efficiently, Cutter Boley will carry more of the burden after throwing for 1,040 yards with 10 touchdowns and three interceptions through four games. Reed Harris gives him a legitimate playmaker after producing 499 receiving yards and five touchdowns over that span.

The useful question is whether Arizona State can turn completions into drive-changing gains before Texas Tech ends possessions. The Sun Devils averaged 8.1 yards after the catch per reception through four games, so there is some volatility here. If Texas Tech contains that element, our projected game script becomes much easier to reach.
