For us, the case for UAB is less about trusting its offense to control the game and more about expecting its defense to keep the margin compressed. It also held Louisiana scoreless over its final six possessions. We are not treating that short stretch as proof, but it supports our view that UAB can remain competitive without needing a big offensive performance.

That matters because settling for field goals places more pressure on the defense. We think the market is placing more weight on UAB's offensive limitations than our overall numbers warrant.
