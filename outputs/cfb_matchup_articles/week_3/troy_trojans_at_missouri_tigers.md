## Our Take

The Troy Trojans visit the Missouri Tigers at Memorial Stadium. We make **Troy +24.5**, compared with a market line of +27.5.

The best number we found is Troy +27.5 at -110. We give Troy a 55.6% chance to cover, which creates an 3.2% edge for us. That clears our 3% threshold, so Troy is a bet.

We are not dismissing Missouri’s impressive opener. The Tigers won 54-14, produced 562 yards and allowed only 198, while Austin Simmons completed 17 of 19 passes for 252 yards and four touchdowns. That performance showed Missouri’s offensive ceiling, but we are treating one dominant result as context rather than proof that the same margin profile carries forward against Troy.

The more interesting part for us is Missouri’s defense. The 2025 unit allowed just 277.3 yards and 18.9 points per game, but only two defensive starters returned. Our numbers still rate this as a difficult matchup for Troy, particularly through the air, yet the market appears more willing to price Missouri’s defensive continuity as established. We see more uncertainty around a heavily reworked unit than the prior-season results suggest.

Our concern is clear: Troy’s offense grades poorly at creating scoring opportunities, and Missouri is well positioned to force empty possessions. Even so, backing Troy does not require us to argue that the Trojans have the better team. For us, the case is that the market is asking Missouri to turn its matchup advantages into an especially large margin, and our longer-run view stops short of that expectation.

We are watching Troy’s passing offense against Missouri’s pass defense. Our efficiency numbers make that the clearest Missouri advantage: Troy cannot afford repeated passing-down failures because those stalled drives would give Missouri’s offense more chances to create separation.

At the same time, we want to see how Missouri’s rebuilt defense handles a more meaningful sample. The opener was dominant, but with only two returning defensive starters, we are less willing than the market to assume the unit will immediately reproduce last season’s level over a full game.
