The best number we found is North Texas -2.5 at -107. We see a -1.4% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our slight lean toward North Texas comes primarily from the passing matchup. Our numbers rate North Texas among the most efficient passing offenses in the country, while Navy has struggled badly in that area defensively. Tayven Jackson completed 95 of 128 passes for 1,187 yards through four games, and Justin Stevenson and Corri Milliner had each surpassed 275 receiving yards. We see multiple ways for North Texas to challenge the part of Navy’s defense our numbers trust least.

Navy averaged 376 rushing yards and 6.90 yards per carry through its first two games, while North Texas allowed 337 rushing yards to Texas State. We are not treating either small sample as proof, but that matchup gives Navy a credible path to controlling possessions and keeping the game tight. That is why our disagreement with the market is modest and why the available price does not provide enough value for us.
