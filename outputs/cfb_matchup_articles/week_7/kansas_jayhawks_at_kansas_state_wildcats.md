The best number we found is Kansas +5.5 at -105. We see a -1.6% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

We treat that performance as context rather than proof, especially given the opponent, but the offensive line allowing no sacks and only one tackle for loss is still relevant for a unit with just two returning starters.

For us, that tension explains the narrow model-market disagreement. Kansas has a plausible route through its rushing attack and ability to create scoring chances, but Kansas State’s pass defense can limit the area in which Kansas is less established.
