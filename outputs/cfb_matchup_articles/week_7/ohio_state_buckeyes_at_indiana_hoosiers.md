## Our Take

The Ohio State Buckeyes visit the Indiana Hoosiers at Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN). We make **Indiana +1**, compared with a market line of +3.5.

The best number we found is Indiana +3.5 at -110. We give Indiana a 56.5% chance to cover, which creates an 4.1% edge for us. That clears our 3% threshold, so Indiana is a bet.

We are not dismissing Ohio State’s offensive start. The Buckeyes averaged 45.0 points and 522.0 yards through four games, while Jeremiah Smith had already produced 617 receiving yards and seven touchdowns. That passing-game ceiling is the clearest concern for our Indiana position, and it explains why the market is comfortable pricing Ohio State as the stronger side.

For us, the disagreement starts with Indiana’s ability to challenge Ohio State on the ground. Our numbers rate Indiana’s rush defense far better than Ohio State’s rushing offense, creating a path for the Hoosiers to make the Buckeyes more one-dimensional. We also see more uncertainty in Ohio State’s defense than the early results suggest: only three defensive starters returned after eight were lost from the 2025 unit. Ohio State allowed just 12.3 points per game through four games, but our longer-run view does not treat that small sample as definitive. That combination gives us enough reason to support Indiana at the available price.
