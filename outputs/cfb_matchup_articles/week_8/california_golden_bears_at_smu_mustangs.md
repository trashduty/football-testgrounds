### The Matchup To Watch

We are watching California’s scoring-opportunity creation against SMU’s defense. California’s recent passing total looks impressive, but Taimane Purcell’s 91-yard touchdown accounted for a significant piece of it. The useful question is whether California can produce sustained drives rather than depend on isolated explosive plays.

If SMU contains those explosives and forces California to generate repeated successful plays, the underlying efficiency matchup favors the home side. If California continues finishing drives efficiently and creates short fields through takeaways, staying within the margin becomes more realistic.
