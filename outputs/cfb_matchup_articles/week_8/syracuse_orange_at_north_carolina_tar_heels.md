## Our Take

The Syracuse Orange visit the North Carolina Tar Heels at Kenan Stadium. We make **Syracuse +9**, compared with a market line of +8.5.

The best number we found is Syracuse +9.5 at -105. We see a 1.6% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

We are not treating Syracuse’s opening rout as proof that its offense has solved everything. Still, we saw useful signs in Steve Angeli’s return: he threw for 263 yards, while Umari Hatcher produced eight catches for 147 yards and two touchdowns. Syracuse also received strong pass protection in that game. Those developments help explain why our numbers are somewhat more receptive to Syracuse than the market appears to be.

The concern is whether that production carries over against a much more demanding matchup. Syracuse’s longer-run passing efficiency and ability to create scoring opportunities compare poorly with North Carolina’s defensive profile, while North Carolina’s passing offense has an efficiency advantage against the Syracuse pass defense. Syracuse generated five sacks and nine tackles for loss in its opener, but we need more evidence before treating that performance as the new baseline. For us, the current price does not create enough separation from the market to justify a wager.
