## Our Take

The LSU Tigers visit the Auburn Tigers at Jordan-Hare Stadium. We make **Auburn +7**, compared with a market line of +7.5.

The best number we found is Auburn +7.5 at -110. We see a 1.8% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our modest lean toward Auburn starts with its ability to keep LSU from controlling the game on the ground. Auburn allowed 3.1 yards per carry through four games, while its defense generated 11 sacks and five interceptions. That matters against an LSU offense that has produced impressive early yardage but has also thrown four interceptions. We see enough potential disruption for Auburn to shorten possessions and prevent LSU from separating cleanly.

The concern for us is Auburn’s passing offense against LSU’s pass defense. LSU recorded 12 sacks through two games and allowed opponents to convert only four of 27 third downs. Auburn also managed only 279 total yards against Vanderbilt, so we are not assuming it can consistently sustain drives here. Byrum Brown’s mobility provides another path—he had 237 rushing yards through four games—but Auburn still faces the most difficult unit-level matchup in this game.

That tension explains our view. Auburn’s run defense and Brown’s dual-threat ability give the home side a plausible route to staying close, but LSU’s pressure and coverage can create the negative plays needed for separation. Our disagreement with the market is therefore too narrow to justify a wager at the available price.

### The Matchup To Watch

We are watching Auburn’s passing offense against LSU’s pass defense. Auburn converted half of its third downs through four games, but LSU’s early third-down defense and pass rush have been disruptive, with Princewill Umanmielen producing five sacks and 6.5 tackles for loss through two games. If Auburn can protect Brown and preserve his run-pass flexibility, it can sustain enough offense to remain competitive. If LSU consistently forces obvious passing situations, the matchup shifts sharply toward the favorite.
