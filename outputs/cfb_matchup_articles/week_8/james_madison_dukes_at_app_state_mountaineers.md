The James Madison Dukes visit the App State Mountaineers at Kidd Brewer Stadium. We make **James Madison -13**, compared with a market line of -11.5.

The best number we found is James Madison -10.5 at -110. We see a -0.3% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our disagreement with the market starts on the ground. We see James Madison holding an advantage in both rushing matchups: its offense has been efficient against a vulnerable App State run defense, while its own run defense presents a significant challenge for an App State rushing attack that has struggled. James Madison averaged 219.3 rushing yards through six games, and George Pettaway led the team with 648 yards and six touchdowns on 95 carries. That gives us a credible path to sustained control rather than one dependent on isolated explosive plays.

The main concern for us is that App State may have more opportunity through the air. James Madison allowed 245 passing yards per game compared with only 102 on the ground, so the matchup is not restrictive in every area. Still, our numbers favor James Madison's ability to run, prevent App State from doing the same and create scoring opportunities more consistently. That explains why our expectation is stronger than the market's, but the difference is not large enough to provide sufficient value at the available price. We pass.
