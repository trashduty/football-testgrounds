## Our Take

The Louisville Cardinals visit the Syracuse Orange at JMA Wireless Dome. We make **Syracuse +13**, compared with a market line of +13.5.

The best number we found is Syracuse +13.5 at -105. We see a 1.6% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our numbers are slightly more favorable toward Syracuse than the market, largely because we see enough stability in the passing game to keep the offense competitive. Steve Angeli returned from his 2025 season-ending injury and threw for 263 yards in the opener, while Umari Hatcher produced career highs of eight catches, 147 yards and two touchdowns. We also saw Syracuse's offensive line earn an 87.0 pass-blocking grade from PFF. Those results came against New Hampshire, so we are treating them as encouraging context rather than proof against a much stronger opponent.

The concern for us is whether Syracuse can prevent Louisville from controlling the game on the ground. Our longer-run metrics give Louisville a substantial advantage in rushing efficiency, and that could create repeated scoring opportunities while limiting Syracuse's possessions. Syracuse held New Hampshire to 60 rushing yards, but one opener does not erase the broader concern. We can see a path for Syracuse to remain competitive through Angeli and the passing game, but the price does not give us enough separation from our expectation to make this a wager.

If Louisville consistently generates efficient runs, Syracuse could be forced to defend long drives while its offense plays from less favorable game states. We saw Syracuse's rebuilt front produce five sacks in the opener—one-quarter of its entire 2025 total—but run defense is the more important test here, and our numbers still make it the clearest pressure point.
