For us, the disagreement is less about declaring Sacramento State the better team and more about whether Ball State can create enough scoring opportunities to justify being favored by this much. Our numbers pair a struggling Ball State opportunity-creation profile with a Sacramento State defense that has been much better at preventing those chances. At Northwestern, Ball State produced 360 yards and 22 first downs while going three-and-out only twice, yet finished with 14 points. We are not treating one result as proof, but it illustrates the gap between moving the ball and creating scoreboard separation.

Even so, our broader matchup numbers support Sacramento State staying competitive enough for our position.
