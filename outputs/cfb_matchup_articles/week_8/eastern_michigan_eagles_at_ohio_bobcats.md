## Our Take

The Eastern Michigan Eagles visit the Ohio Bobcats at Peden Stadium. We make **Eastern Michigan +13**, compared with a market line of +12.

The best number we found is Eastern Michigan +12.5 at -110. We see a -2.0% edge there, but that does not clear our 3% threshold, so we are passing.

Our model sees a narrow case for Eastern Michigan staying competitive, built more around offensive stability than a clear matchup advantage. Noah Kim had completed 106 of 173 passes for 970 yards with six touchdowns and three interceptions through five games, while Benson Prosper and Harold Mack Jr. had emerged as his leading targets. Eastern Michigan also ranked first in the MAC in fewest penalties and penalty yards per game, which matters for an underdog trying to avoid giving away possessions.

The Lindenwood game offered some encouraging context: Eastern Michigan scored three touchdowns, attempted four field goals and did not have a three-and-out across eight possessions. We are not treating one result as proof of an offensive breakthrough, especially because the broader efficiency profile still raises concerns about creating scoring opportunities consistently.

The bigger issue is the ground-game matchup on both sides. Ohio owns a substantial rushing-efficiency advantage against Eastern Michigan's defense, while Eastern Michigan's rushing offense faces a strong Ohio run defense. That combination gives Ohio a credible path to control the game, and our expectation remains too close to the market to justify paying the current price on Eastern Michigan.

### The Matchup To Watch

For us, the matchup to watch is Ohio's rushing offense against Eastern Michigan's run defense. Eastern Michigan had improved its pressure production to nine sacks through five games, but disrupting the passing game is different from consistently limiting an efficient rushing attack. If Ohio remains productive on the ground, Eastern Michigan may have to rely heavily on Kim and the passing game to keep pace. That concern is a major reason the small model disagreement does not become a wager.
