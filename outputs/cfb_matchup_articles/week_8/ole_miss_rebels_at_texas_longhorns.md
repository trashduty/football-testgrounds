### The Matchup To Watch

We are watching Ole Miss’ pass protection against Texas edge Colin Simmons. Ole Miss had allowed only one sack per game while operating at 23.1 seconds per play, so its tempo and protection have helped Chambliss stay productive. Simmons led the SEC with 12 sacks in 2025 and had recorded a sack in seven of his previous eight games entering this matchup.

The question is whether Ole Miss can keep that structure intact against Texas’ best pressure threat. If we see Chambliss consistently getting the ball out on schedule, the matchup tilts toward the strongest part of the Ole Miss offense. If Simmons creates negative plays without Texas having to manufacture pressure, the passing-game advantage our numbers identify becomes much harder to realize.
